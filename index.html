About

When a flood or a war hits, local clinics and health organizations are often the first to respond and the last to recover. They rarely have time for a website, a social media plan, or a better way to track donors.

That's where we come in. Daybreak Collective puts student teams to work on the practical problems these organizations face: branding, marketing, digital tools, and putting AI to use in day-to-day operations.

We started as the Global Development Club at The Nueva School in San Mateo, California. Today we have student-run chapters at high schools in three U.S. states and three countries, and we work with organizations around the world.

Our partners get real help, regardless of their size or location. Our members get hands-on experience working alongside business and nonprofit leaders.

What we do

Three ways we help

Every project starts with a conversation about what our partner actually needs. These are the areas we come back to most.

01

Digital presence

Getting found and trusted online, so more people can reach the care and services our partners provide.

  • Websites and online stores
  • Branding and logo design
  • Social media and video content
02

Technology & AI

Lightweight tools that free up staff time, plus training so teams can keep using them after we hand off.

  • Automating budgeting and logistics
  • Volunteer and donor sign-up tracking
  • Seminars on using AI to scale
03

Funding

Raising money through student-run events and directing it where it does the most good.

  • Fundraising events
  • Micro-grants for early-stage ventures
  • Support for partner organizations
